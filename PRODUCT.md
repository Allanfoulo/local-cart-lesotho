# Mabote Fresh

## Register

product

## Users
Residents of Maseru buying everyday essentials on phones, including first-time online shoppers and older customers. Shop staff manage fulfilment primarily on desktop. Customers may be outdoors in bright daylight on lower-end phones.

## Product Purpose
A single local grocery shop with a complete browse, cart, guest checkout, tracking and reorder journey. Staff manage the same orders and catalogue. The approved deliverable is a browser-persisted prototype with explicit boundaries for future backend, authentication and payment integrations.

## Brand Personality
Local, friendly, dependable. Mabote Fresh: good food, brighter tomorrows.

## Anti-references
Corporate SaaS landing pages, dark futuristic interfaces, glass effects, tiny controls and complicated nested menus.

## Design Principles
Shopping comes first. Show prices and selling units clearly. Keep checkout short and reversible before submission. Make stock, payment and delivery states explicit. Keep customer and staff workflows connected.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable daylight contrast, labelled forms, visible keyboard focus, generous touch targets, reduced-motion support and responsive layouts.
